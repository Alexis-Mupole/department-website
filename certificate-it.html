Program Overview

The Certificate in Information Technology is a comprehensive program recognized by the Uganda Business and Technical Examinations Board (UBTEB). This program provides foundational IT skills and knowledge that prepare students for entry-level positions in the technology sector or for further studies.

The UBTEB certification is nationally recognized and valued by employers across Uganda, providing graduates with a competitive edge in the job market.

Core Subjects

Computer Fundamentals Office Applications Internet Basics Introduction to Programming Database Basics IT Support
