Welcome Message

"It is my distinct honor to welcome you to the Department of Computing & Informatics. Our department is committed to providing high-quality education that prepares students for the dynamic and ever-evolving world of technology."

With extensive experience in IT education, I have overseen the growth of our department into one of the most respected computing programs in East Africa. Our comprehensive programs combine theoretical knowledge with practical skills, emphasizing innovation, ethical practice, and collaborative learning.
