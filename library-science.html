Program Overview

The Bachelor of Library and Information Science program prepares students to manage information resources, libraries, and archives effectively. This program combines traditional library science with modern information technology to produce professionals who can organize, preserve, and disseminate information in the digital age.

Students learn about information systems, digital curation, knowledge management, and user services, preparing them for diverse roles in information-intensive organizations.

Core Subjects

Information Organization Digital Libraries Cataloging & Classification Records Management Knowledge Management Information Ethics

Curriculum Overview

What you'll learn throughout the program

Year 1

Semester 1

Introduction to Library Science, Computer Fundamentals, Communication Skills, Study Skills

Semester 2

Information Sources, Library Administration, Introduction to Databases, Office Applications

Year 2

Semester 1

Cataloging & Classification, Information Literacy, Reference Services, Statistics

Semester 2

Digital Libraries, Library Automation, Research Methods, Publishing

Year 3

Semester 1

Records Management, Knowledge Management, Information Systems, Archival Studies

Semester 2

Information Ethics, Digital Preservation, Web Content Management, Elective

Year 4

Semester 1

Information Policy, Library Management, Metadata Standards, Elective II

Semester 2

Final Year Project, Practicum, Information Technology in Libraries

Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (A-Level)

  • UCE with at least 5 passes
  • UACE with at least 2 principal passes
  • English is essential
  • Any subject combination accepted

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Relevant work experience in libraries
