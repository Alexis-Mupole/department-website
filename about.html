Who We Are

About the Department

A centre of excellence in computing education, research, and community service in East Africa.

Our Mission

To provide world-class computing education that equips students with technical excellence, critical thinking, and ethical values to drive technological innovation in Uganda and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computing education, producing graduates who are competitive in the global job market.

Our Values

  • Excellence in Education
  • Innovation & Creativity
  • Integrity & Ethics
  • Collaboration
  • Service to Community

Accreditation

Our programs are accredited by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) Uganda and aligned with international computing standards.

Community Impact

We engage with local communities through outreach programs, digital literacy campaigns, and technology workshops.
