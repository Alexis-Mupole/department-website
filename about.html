About Bugema University

Bugema University is a prestigious private Christian university located in Uganda, committed to academic excellence, spiritual development, and community service.

📍 Location

Bugema University is situated in Kalagala Sub-County, Luwero District, approximately 70 kilometers north of Kampala, Uganda's capital city. The campus provides a serene and conducive environment for learning.

📅 Establishment

Founded in 1993, Bugema University has grown to become one of Uganda's leading private universities, offering a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various faculties.

🎓 Accreditation

All programs are accredited by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) in Uganda. Our degrees are recognized internationally, enabling graduates to pursue further studies or employment abroad.

👨‍🎓 Student Population

With over 5,000 students enrolled across various programs, Bugema University fosters a diverse and inclusive learning community drawn from across Uganda and the East African region.

About the Department

The Department of Computing and Technology is one of the most dynamic and rapidly growing departments at Bugema University. Established to meet the growing demand for skilled IT professionals in Uganda and the broader East African region, our department has produced hundreds of graduates who are now making significant contributions in various sectors.
