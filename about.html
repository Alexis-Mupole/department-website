Department of Computing & Informatics

Welcome to the Department of Computing & Informatics at Bugema University. We are part of the School of Science & Technology, committed to providing high-quality education that prepares students for the fast-evolving world of computing and information technology.

📍 Location

Bugema University is situated in Kalagala Sub-County, Luwero District, approximately 70 kilometers north of Kampala, Uganda's capital city. The campus provides a serene and conducive environment for learning.

📅 Establishment

Founded in 1993, Bugema University has grown to become one of Uganda's leading private universities. The Department of Computing & Informatics has been at the forefront of producing skilled IT professionals.

🎓 Accreditation

All programs are accredited by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE) in Uganda. Our degrees are recognized internationally across Africa and beyond.

✝️ Our Values

Rooted in Seventh-day Adventist values, our programs blend cutting-edge technical skills with ethical leadership, preparing students to contribute positively to society.

Message from the Head of Department

Welcome to the Department of Computing & Informatics. As the Head of Department, I am proud to lead a department committed to providing high-quality education that prepares students for the fast-evolving world of computing and information technology.

Our programs are designed to equip students with strong theoretical knowledge and practical skills in areas such as software development, data management, networking, cybersecurity, and information systems. We emphasize problem solving, innovation, ethical practice, and teamwork to ensure our graduates are ready to meet the demands of the modern digital economy.

Our experienced faculty support students throughout their academic journey, encouraging research, creativity, and community engagement. We invite you to explore our programs and join us in shaping competent professionals who can drive technological innovation and contribute positively to society.

Mr. Ssewankambo Erma

Head of Department

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To produce highly skilled computing professionals who can contribute meaningfully to national and global technological development through innovative education, research, and ethical practice rooted in Christian values.

🔭 Our Vision
