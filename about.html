Academic Programs We Offer

Our department offers a comprehensive range of computing programs designed to meet the diverse needs of the tech industry:

  • BSc. Information Technology - Software engineering, cybersecurity, networking, and data analytics
  • BSc. Business Computing - Computing skills with business knowledge for the digital economy
  • BSc. Network Systems Administration - Design, implement, and manage enterprise networks
  • BSc. Data Science & AI - Data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence
  • BSc. Library & Information Science - Manage information resources and digital archives
  • Diploma in Computer Engineering - Practical skills in hardware and system maintenance
  • Certificate in IT (UBTEB) - Entry-level certification recognized by UBTEB

Admission Requirements

Join our community of future tech professionals. Here's what you need to apply:

Bachelor's Programs

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes
  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 2 principal passes
  • English and Mathematics recommended at UACE level
  • Any relevant subject combination accepted

Diploma Programs

  • UCE with at least 5 passes
  • Relevant certificate accepted for mature entry

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Relevant work experience in IT field
