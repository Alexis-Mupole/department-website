Welcome to Computing & Informatics

Part of the School of Science & Technology at Bugema University

📍 Location

Bugema University is located in Kalagala Sub-County, Luwero District, approximately 70km north of Kampala. Our campus provides a serene environment for learning.

📅 Our History

Founded in 1993, Bugema University has grown to become one of Uganda's leading private universities. The Department of Computing & Informatics has been producing skilled IT professionals.

🎓 Accreditation

All programs are accredited by the National Council for Higher Education (NCHE). Our degrees are recognized across Africa and internationally.

✝️ Our Values

Rooted in Seventh-day Adventist values, we blend cutting-edge technical skills with ethical leadership, preparing students to contribute positively to society.

Welcome to the Department of Computing & Informatics. I am proud to lead a department committed to providing high-quality education that prepares students for the fast-evolving world of computing and information technology.

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To produce highly skilled computing professionals who contribute meaningfully to national and global technological development through innovative education, research, and ethical practice rooted in Christian values.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in computing education and research, producing graduates who are competitive in the global job market and committed to using technology for positive societal impact.
