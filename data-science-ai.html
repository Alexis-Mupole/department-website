Program Overview

The BSc. Applied Data Science & Artificial Intelligence program equips students with cutting-edge skills in data science, machine learning, and AI technologies. This program prepares graduates to extract insights from data and build intelligent systems that drive decision-making in modern organizations.

Students learn to work with large datasets, build predictive models, and develop AI solutions using industry-standard tools and frameworks.

Core Subjects

Data Science Machine Learning Artificial Intelligence Data Visualization Deep Learning Statistics

Curriculum Overview

What you'll learn throughout the program

Year 1

Semester 1

Computer Fundamentals, Introduction to Programming, Mathematics I, Statistics, Communication Skills

Semester 2

Data Structures, Calculus, Linear Algebra, Python Programming, Office Applications

Year 2

Semester 1

Data Science Foundations, Database Management, Probability Theory, Machine Learning Basics

Semester 2

Data Analysis with Python, Statistics for Data Science, Data Visualization, Algorithms

Year 3

Semester 1

Machine Learning, Big Data Technologies, Neural Networks, Feature Engineering

Semester 2

Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Research Methods

Year 4

Semester 1

AI Systems Design, Reinforcement Learning, Cloud AI Services, Elective I

Semester 2

Final Year Project, AI Ethics, MLOps, Industry Attachment

Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (A-Level)

  • UCE with at least 5 passes
  • UACE with at least 2 principal passes
  • Mathematics is essential
  • Physics or Economics recommended

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Strong mathematical background
