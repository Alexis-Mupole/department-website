Core Subjects

Year Semester Subjects
Year 1 Semester 1 Introduction to Business, Computer Fundamentals, Mathematics I, Communication Skills, Economics I
Semester 2 Introduction to Programming, Business Mathematics, Office Applications, Economics II, Accounting I
Year 2 Semester 1 Database Management, Business Statistics, Principles of Management, Financial Accounting
Semester 2 Web Development for Business, Business Information Systems, Marketing Principles, Accounting II
Year 3 Semester 1 E-Business, Project Management, Human Resource Management, Data Analysis
Semester 2 Enterprise Systems, IT Governance, Business Process Management, Research Methods
Year 4 Semester 1 Strategic IT Management, Business Intelligence, Innovation Management, Elective I
Semester 2 Final Year Project, IT Ethics and Law, Entrepreneurship, Elective II

Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (A-Level)

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes
  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 2 principal passes
  • English and a relevant subject combination

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Relevant work experience
