Program Overview

The Bachelor of Business Computing bridges the gap between business management and information technology. This program equips students with skills in e-commerce, business analytics, and systems design to drive digital transformation in organizations.

Students learn to align technology solutions with business objectives, making them valuable assets in any organization seeking to leverage IT for competitive advantage.

Core Subjects

Business Information Systems Database Management Project Management E-Business Data Analysis IT Governance

Curriculum Overview

What you'll learn throughout the program

Year 1

Semester 1

Computer Fundamentals, Introduction to Programming, Business Mathematics, Communication Skills, Principles of Management

Semester 2

Business Information Systems, Office Applications, Statistics, Introduction to Accounting, Internet Technologies

Year 2

Semester 1

Database Management, Object-Oriented Programming, Business Law, Economics, Systems Analysis & Design

Semester 2

E-Business, Web Development, Project Management, Financial Management, Operating Systems

Year 3

Semester 1

Data Analysis, Enterprise Resource Planning, Network Fundamentals, Business Intelligence, Marketing Management

Semester 2

IT Governance, E-commerce Development, Decision Support Systems, Research Methods, Human Resource Management

Year 4

Semester 1

Strategic IT Management, Cloud Computing, Elective I, Risk Management, Business Ethics

Semester 2

Final Year Project, IT Audit, Digital Transformation, Elective II, Industry Attachment

Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (A-Level)

  • UCE with at least 5 passes
  • UACE with at least 2 principal passes
  • English and Mathematics recommended
  • Any relevant subject combination

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Relevant work experience
