FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

For bachelor's programs, you need UCE with at least 5 passes and UACE with 2 principal passes. Mathematics at O-Level is recommended. Diploma holders may also apply for credit transfer.

How do I apply?

You can apply online through our Application Form, visit the admissions office in person, or email us for the application form. The process is simple and we guide you through every step.

What is the tuition fee?

Tuition varies by program. Contact the admissions office for the latest fee structure. We offer flexible payment plans to help you manage your education expenses.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships and financial aid to qualifying students. Contact the admissions office for more information about available scholarships.
