Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

For bachelor's programs, you need UCE with at least 5 passes and UACE with 2 principal passes. Mathematics at O-Level is required for some programs.

How do I apply?

You can apply online through our website, visit the admissions office in person, or download the application form and submit it via email.

What is the tuition fee?

Tuition varies by program. BSc. programs cost approximately UGX 2,800,000 per year. Contact the admissions office for the latest fee structure.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships and financial aid to qualifying students. Contact the admissions office for more information.
