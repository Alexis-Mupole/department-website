Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (A-Level)

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes
  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 2 principal passes
  • English and Mathematics recommended at UACE
  • Any relevant subject combination

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Relevant work experience in IT field
