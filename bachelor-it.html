Core Subjects

Year Semester Subjects
Year 1 Semester 1 Computer Fundamentals, Introduction to Programming, Mathematics I, Communication Skills, Physics
Semester 2 Data Structures, Mathematics II, Office Applications, Introduction to Internet, Electronics
Year 2 Semester 1 Object-Oriented Programming, Database Management, Computer Architecture, Statistics
Semester 2 Web Development, Operating Systems, Computer Networks I, Software Engineering I
Year 3 Semester 1 Advanced Web Development, Database Administration, Computer Networks II, Cybersecurity Fundamentals
Semester 2 Mobile App Development, Software Engineering II, Cloud Computing, Research Methods
Year 4 Semester 1 Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Project Management, Elective I
Semester 2 Final Year Project, IT Ethics, Enterprise Systems, Elective II

Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (A-Level)

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes
  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 2 principal passes
  • English and Mathematics recommended at UACE
  • Any relevant subject combination

Mature Age Entry

  • At least 25 years of age
  • Passed mature age entrance examination
  • Relevant work experience in IT field
