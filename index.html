Message from Our Head

👨‍🏫

Mr. Ssewankambo Erma

Head of Department

Welcome to the Department of Computing & Informatics. I am proud to lead a department committed to providing high-quality education that prepares students for the fast-evolving world of computing and information technology.

Our programs are designed to equip students with strong theoretical knowledge and practical skills in software development, data management, networking, cybersecurity, and information systems. We emphasize problem-solving, innovation, ethical practice, and teamwork.

Join us to shape competent professionals who can drive technological innovation and contribute positively to society.
