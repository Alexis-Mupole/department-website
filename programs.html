Programs Offered

The Department of Computing & Informatics offers a wide range of accredited programs under the School of Science & Technology. Our programs are designed to prepare students for successful careers in the technology industry.

Bachelor of Information Technology

Most Popular

Our Bachelor of Information Technology program empowers students to navigate and shape the dynamic digital landscape. Rooted in Seventh-day Adventist values, this program blends cutting-edge skills in software engineering, cybersecurity, networking, and data analytics with ethical leadership tailored to Africa's digital needs.

Career Opportunities:

  • Software Developer
  • Network Administrator
  • Database Administrator
  • IT Project Manager
  • Systems Analyst
  • Cybersecurity Analyst

Core Subjects:

Software Engineering Cybersecurity Data Analytics Networking Web Development Database Management

Bachelor of Business Computing

This program combines computing skills with business knowledge to produce professionals who can bridge the gap between technology and business operations. Students learn to apply IT solutions to business challenges.

Career Opportunities:

  • Business Analyst
  • IT Consultant
  • Systems Analyst
  • Database Manager
  • E-commerce Specialist
  • IT Manager

Core Subjects:

Business Information Systems Database Management Project Management E-Business Data Analysis IT Governance

Bachelor of Science in Network Systems Administration

This program prepares students to design, implement, and manage enterprise network infrastructure. Students gain hands-on experience with routers, switches, firewalls, and cloud platforms.

Career Opportunities:

  • Network Administrator
  • Systems Administrator
  • Network Engineer
  • IT Support Specialist
  • Cloud Administrator
  • Security Analyst

Core Subjects:

Network Design Server Administration Cloud Computing Network Security Virtualization Wireless Networks

Admission Requirements

Bachelor's Programs

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes
  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 2 principal passes
  • English and Mathematics at O-Level are recommended

Diploma Programs

  • UCE certificate with at least 5 passes
  • Relevant certificate or experience may be considered

Certificate Program

  • UCE certificate
  • Basic computer knowledge
  • Interest in information technology
